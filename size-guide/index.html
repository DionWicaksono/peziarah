Measurements are of the body, not the garment. Pieces marked One size — hijabs, sarongs, hats and totes — fit everyone the same way.

How to measure

Three numbers, one tape

  • Bust — around the fullest part, tape level under the arms.
  • Waist — around the narrowest part, usually just above the navel.
  • Hip — around the fullest part, feet together.
Fit notes

How the cloth behaves

  • Linen softens and relaxes about half a size with wear and washing.
  • Crochet and knit pieces stretch slightly; bikinis are cut true to size.
  • Our model is 172 cm and wears M unless a product page says otherwise.
