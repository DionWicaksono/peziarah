Chapter I — The House

We cut for the wet-haired hour, not the runway.

Toi, Moi et la Mer began with one length of cloth off a Gianyar loom and a question — what do you actually wear between the pool and the table? Everything since has answered it: wrap it, tie it, throw it on damp, leave it creased.

Nineteen pieces, dyed with clay and madder, sewn by nine people we know by name. No season after this one — when a run sells out, the loom moves on.

Chapter IV — The Atelier

Four villages, nine pairs of hands.

Warp wound in Gianyar. Clay ground and vatted in Singaraja. Raffia crocheted in Sidemen. Everything cut and finished in a two-room studio in Canggu, a ten-minute ride from the water it was made for.
