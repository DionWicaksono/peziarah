Help

Questions, answered

The ones we hear most. Anything else, the studio is a message away.

How do I place an order?+

Add pieces to your bag and tap “Order on WhatsApp”. Your order opens as a pre-filled message to the studio; we confirm stock and size, then send payment details.

How do I pay?+

In Indonesian rupiah, by bank transfer or a secure payment link we send in the chat. Prices on the site can be shown in USD for reference.

Can you deliver to my villa?+

Yes — same-day scooter delivery anywhere in Bali for orders confirmed before 18:00 WITA. Choose “Direct to your villa” in the bag.

Do you ship outside Indonesia?+

On request. Tell us your country in the chat and we'll quote tracked shipping before you pay.

What if it doesn't fit?+

Exchange it for another size or return it unworn within 30 days. See Shipping & returns.

Which size should I take?+

Our cuts are relaxed and linen gives about half a size. Check the size guide or send us your measurements.

How do I care for linen?+

Cold wash, dry in the shade, skip the iron. The creases are part of it.

Can I visit the studio?+

Yes, by appointment — Jalan Pantai Batu Bolong, Canggu. Book through the contact page.
